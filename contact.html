Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed within 24 hours. Local delivery within Accra takes 1-2 business days. International shipping spans 5-10 business days depending on the courier.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of the delivery date, provided the items are unworn and in their original packaging. Custom pieces are non-refundable.

How do I track my order?

Once logged into your account, navigate to the Dashboard to view your Order History and real-time tracking status.
